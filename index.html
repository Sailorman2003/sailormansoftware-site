The story behind La Tavola

It started with a question at our dinner table.

My wife, Mafalda, loves inviting friends to dinner. But she wanted a better way to remember who had come, what she'd served, and what everyone liked.

After a long career in software—and twenty years away from programming—I built La Tavola to solve that very human problem. Today it helps with everything from keeping dinner-party memories to planning everyday meals.

Made for hosts, by someone who knows why the details matter.

“The best part of a dinner party is being together. The details shouldn't get in the way.”
