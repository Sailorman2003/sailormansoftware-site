Made for intimate home entertaining. Not for catering, and not for event companies.

Group conversations in Apple Messages

Keep the whole table in the conversation.

From any dinner party, tap Message and La Tavola starts a new group conversation in Apple Messages with the guests you choose: everyone you invited, or only those attending, those who said maybe, those who declined, or those who haven’t replied yet.

Talk over the menu, sort out who is bringing what, and change plans together. You write the message yourself; La Tavola only addresses it. Your guests reply in Messages as they always do, and they don’t need La Tavola.

Questions

Is there an app that remembers what I served each guest?

Yes. That is why La Tavola was made. Every dinner party you give is kept with its guests and its menu, so when you invite the same friends again you can see what they were served last time.

How do I keep track of my guests’ dietary restrictions?

Record each guest’s food preferences and dislikes. Choose guests before recipes, and La Tavola flags ingredients that conflict with those recorded preferences as you build the menu. It does not verify food safety or manage allergies.

Can I message all my guests at once?

Yes. From a dinner party, La Tavola starts a new group conversation in Apple Messages with everyone invited, or only the guests who are attending, said maybe, declined or haven’t replied. You write the message, and guests reply in Messages without needing the app.

Can it turn a cookbook page into a recipe?

Yes. Photograph the page, even a recipe that runs over several pages, and La Tavola reads it into a recipe you can edit. It can also import a recipe from a website or from pasted text.

Does it scale recipes for the number of guests?

Yes. Recipes scale to the number of guests who have accepted, and the shopping list uses the scaled quantities.

How much does La Tavola cost?

It is free to download, and it starts with a free trial. After that, a subscription is $9.99 a year in the US. One subscription covers your iPhone and your iPad. There is no monthly plan.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad, with iOS or iPadOS 15 or later. There is no Android version.

Where are my recipes and guest lists kept?

On your own devices, and in your own iCloud account if you sync an iPhone and an iPad. They are not stored on our servers. Guest names, contact details and dietary preferences are never sent to us or to the AI.

Is it for caterers or event planners?

No. La Tavola is made for people who cook for friends and family at home.
